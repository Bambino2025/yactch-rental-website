The fleet's pocket rocket

The Yamaha 255XD is the smallest, quickest boat in our fleet — a 25-foot jet boat built for groups who want speed, spray and sandbar time rather than a floating lounge. Jet propulsion means shallow-water agility, so your captain can tuck into spots on Biscayne Bay that the big yachts pass by.

It's the right call for a fast skyline tour, a watersports afternoon, or an add-on chase boat alongside a larger charter from the fleet. Want more deck for the same group? The 28' Axopar Brabus and 46' Maxum are the next steps up.
