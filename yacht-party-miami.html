Your own yacht. Your own party.

A yacht party in Miami should not mean queuing for a ticketed booze cruise with a hundred strangers. When you book with Miami Yacht Collective, the entire yacht is yours: your people, your playlist on the sound system, your route through Biscayne Bay. We run a hand-picked fleet of ten vessels from 25 to 90 feet, every one chartered with a licensed captain and professional crew, so you host the party and we handle the water.

Groups celebrate everything on our decks — birthdays, bachelorette weekends, engagements, graduations, promotions, or a Saturday that deserves better than a rooftop. If you can toast to it, it belongs on the bay.

How a private yacht party works

Step 1

Call or WhatsApp

Tell us your date, group size and the occasion. We answer 24/7 and check live availability while you’re on the line.

Step 2

We match the yacht

We recommend the right boat from our 10-vessel fleet for your group and budget — no platforms, no broker markup.

Step 3

Step aboard

Meet us at our Miami River dock — about 10 minutes from Brickell and 15 from Miami Beach — and your captain takes it from there.

Where your party cruises

The classic Miami party route leaves our dock on the Miami River, slides past the downtown and Brickell skyline, and opens into Biscayne Bay. From there your captain loops the celebrity mansions of Star Island and Fisher Island, then drops anchor at a sandbar — Nixon's is the crowd favorite — so everyone can swim, float and take the photos that end up framed. Sunset slots put the skyline glow behind the last hours of the party; night runs turn the city lights into your backdrop.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a yacht party in Miami cost?

Private yacht parties with us start at $899 for 4 hours on the 46' Maxum. Most groups choose a 55–75 ft yacht at $1,600–$2,200 for 4 hours, and our 90 ft superyachts start at $2,500–$3,000. Every price includes a licensed captain and crew — see the full Miami yacht rental price guide.

Is this a ticketed party boat?

No. This is a fully private charter: the whole yacht is yours, with just your group on board. You choose the music, the route and the vibe — no strangers, no ticket lines.

Where does the party go?

Most parties cruise Biscayne Bay past Star Island and Fisher Island with the Miami skyline behind you, then anchor to swim at a sandbar such as Nixon's. Your captain adjusts the route to your group on the day.
